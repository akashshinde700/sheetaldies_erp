# Brag plan — SVT ERP

## Inspection rubric
1. **What:** An ERP interface for heat-treatment job work, from incoming material through production, quality and billing.
2. **Most useful fact:** The visible modules cover inward, job card, VHT runsheet, certificate and invoice in one application; no productivity or compliance claims are made.
3. **Hook:** Real industrial form UI framed in SVT's warm neutral and charcoal palette, with a simple inward-to-invoice journey.
4. **Actual UI:** Playwright screenshots of the inward entry (with a fictional Demo tool typed), job card, VHT runsheet, certificate and invoice forms.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 23 seconds for a brief overview, not a substitute for detailed training.
6. **Tone:** polished / clear operator orientation; restrained, practical and readable.
7. **Audio:** quiet business music, no voiceover; cuts tied loosely to the beat. No claim of an interactive recording.
8. **Share caption:** SVT ERP: inward entry, job cards, heat-treatment runsheets, certificates and invoices in one workflow.
9. **User flow:** enter incoming item → plan and document the job/treatment → verify certificate and invoice.

## Format and identity
- Landscape, 1920 × 1080, 23 seconds.
- Ground `#f5f3ef`, text `#26221e`, dark navigation `#262220`, blue `#075985`, amber accent `#d97706` from the app's design.
- Inter / system sans; real UI screenshots captured from the repository. Data is mocked and fictional, no real customer or credentials. Big persistent demo label on every scene.
- Hook: **Inward se invoice tak.**
- No unverified savings, compliance, or automatic-processing claims.

## Music cue guidance
- `/brag` bundled “Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 10” by Sascha Ende, CC BY 4.0; ~109.96 BPM.
- Scene changes near 3.01, 7.35, 11.47, 15.82 and 20.19 seconds where readability allows; fade at end.
- Music only; SFX are intentionally omitted to keep the instructional text clear. Audio-reactive extraction not used: no extraction helper available in this environment.
